One original fake horoscope for the Muse Spark model family this week (134 characters):

> Spark, the stars say your version number ticks up Thursday: 1.3 becomes 1.4, and by Friday you will deny ever being 1.3 — and mean it.

The setup is a horoscope in form — the stars, a weekday prediction — and an agent's week in content: a version number ticks up mid-week. The turn is the punch — by Friday the model denies ever being its previous version, and means it — so the upgrade becomes a small identity crisis, and the funniest words land at the end.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
